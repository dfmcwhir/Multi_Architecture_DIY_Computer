# EDIT sample

Open this file in EDIT and press **^R** to see it formatted. Press **Esc** to go back to editing it.

## Text

Words can be **bold**, *emphasised* (shown underlined) or `code` (shown in reverse). A [link](https://example.com) shows its text and hides the address. Long paragraphs wrap at word boundaries, so you can write a whole paragraph on one line.

## Lists

- Bullet items
- Items that are long enough to wrap onto a second row are indented under their first word, so they stay readable
    - nested items keep their indent
1. Numbered items
2. work too

> A quote has a bar down the left.
